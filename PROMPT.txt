## Economics checklist
- Increase = shift right, decrease = shift left. Label old and new (D₀ → D₁) and put a short arrow between them.
- Price below equilibrium → shortage (excess demand) = Qd − Qs on the price line; above → surplus (excess supply). Quantity transacted = the smaller of Qd and Qs.
- Fixed price with a shift: the price stays; brace the old gap (a) and the new gap (b) on the same price line.
- Fixed stock (land, licences, public housing flats, seats, university places) → vertical supply.
- Total revenue / expenditure: price rectangle = |ΔP| × the quantity that stays; quantity rectangle = |ΔQ| × the price that stays. Elastic → flat D; inelastic → steep D. Make the bigger area clearly bigger (about 1.5× or more) and mark the areas "+" and "−".
- Unit tax: S shifts UP by t; consumers pay Pc, producers receive Pc − t; tax revenue = t × new quantity.
- Labour market: y-axis wage rate, x-axis number of workers.
- Negative externality: MSC above MPC; market output Qm > efficient Q*; deadweight loss = triangle between MSC and D from Q* to Qm.
- PPC is concave; inside = unemployment, outside = unattainable; growth = outward shift.
- Diagrams are schematic: numbers from the question ($40, $42.1) are labels on the axis, not to scale; keep the order right.

## dsegraph functions (use only these)
The canvas is 0–100 by 0–100.
- setup("en" or "zh") picks the fonts; T("English", "中文") picks the label for the current language.
- fig, ax = new(3.2, 2.6) — printed size in inches.
- axes(ax, origin, x_end, y_end, xlabel, ylabel) — arrow axes, y title above, x title to the right ("\n" splits a long one).
- Line((x1, y1), (x2, y2)), Line(p, m=slope), Line.vertical(x); L.y(x), L.x(y), L.shift(dx=…, dy=…).
- meet(L1, L2) → the (x, y) intersection.
- draw(ax, L, x0, x1, name, end="right"|"left"|"top"|"bottom", ls="-", dx=…, dy=…); a vertical line: draw(ax, L, y0=…, y1=…, name=…, end="top").
- line(ax, p, q, ls=…), dashed(ax, p, q), curve(ax, xs, ys, ls=…).
- guide(ax, point, origin, ylab, xlab, to_y=True, to_x=True) — dashed lines to both axes with their labels.
- label(ax, x, y, text, ha=…, va=…) — subscripts as r"$P_1$"; never put Chinese inside $…$; write a prime as "S’".
- arrow(ax, p, q), dot(ax, x, y).
- hatch(ax, x0, y0, x1, y1, "////"), dots(ax, x0, y0, x1, y1), region(ax, [points], "xxxx"). Patterns: "////", "....", "xxxx".
- sign(ax, x, y, "+") or sign(ax, x, y, "−") — a label on a white patch; key(ax, x, y, text, "////") — one legend row.
- brace(ax, x0, x1, y, text, side="below"|"above"), vbrace(ax, y0, y1, x, text, side="left"|"right").
- leader(ax, text_xy, target_xy, text) — a label with a thin arrow to what it names.
- save(fig, "diagram_en.png") — also shows and downloads the picture in Colab.

## Pattern (a shortage diagram)
```python
def diagram(lang="en"):
    setup(lang)
    fig, ax = new(3.2, 2.6)
    O = (12, 12)
    axes(ax, O, (88, 12), (12, 90), T("Quantity", "數量"), T("Price", "價格"))

    D = Line((20, 82), (78, 22))
    S = Line((20, 22), (78, 82))
    draw(ax, D, 20, 78, "D", dy=-1.5)
    draw(ax, S, 20, 78, "S", dy=1.5)

    E = meet(D, S)
    guide(ax, E, O, r"$P_e$", to_x=False)

    P = 34                                     # the fixed price, below Pe
    qs, qd = S.x(P), D.x(P)
    line(ax, (O[0], P), (84, P))
    label(ax, O[0] - 1.5, P, "P", ha="right")
    dashed(ax, (qs, O[1]), (qs, P)); label(ax, qs, O[1] - 1.8, r"$Q_s$", va="top")
    dashed(ax, (qd, O[1]), (qd, P)); label(ax, qd, O[1] - 1.8, r"$Q_d$", va="top")
    brace(ax, qs, qd, P - 1, T("shortage", "短缺"), side="below")
    return fig


for lang in ("en", "zh"):
    save(diagram(lang), f"diagram_{lang}.png")
```

## Style
Compute every point with meet / .x() / .y(); never guess one. Move things rather than shrink text. Name each curve at one end. Use a different fill for each area. No label may touch a line or another label, and no dashed guide may run through text; Chinese labels are wider. Black and white only. In Chinese diagrams the curve letters and symbols (D, S, MPC, P₁, E) stay English.
